# Registration

## Fees

* **Early registration** (available through **March 25**):   
  * (Under)graduate students: $100  
  * Postdocs & visiting faculty: $200  
  * Tenure-track faculty & permanently employed: $250  
* **Late registration**, after **March 25**:   
  * (Under)graduate students: $120  
  * Postdocs & visiting faculty: $220  
  * Tenure-track faculty & permanently employed: $270
